# BMH construction design coordination

Read [construction contract](references/construction-contract.md). Consult [source register](references/source-register.md) when selecting standards or checking regulated claims.

1. Locate the current BMH source and project revision. Preserve the existing intake and ten tabs, saved projects, chosen style, material selections and per-floor customer program. Apply the measured drawing and 3D workflows already available.
2. Separate surveyed site data, customer measurements, indicative map geometry, architectural intent and engineering inputs. Carry the source, units, date, coverage and verification state with each assertion. Never infer a legal parcel or buildable area from a map pin or a search summary.
3. Establish what can be drawn now. Use the existing measured architecture for plans, elevations, sections and 3D. Represent a foundation, column, beam, slab or structural wall only from supplied dimensions or an explicitly labelled provisional design. Keep a partition separate from a load-bearing wall. Do not invent soil capacity, loads, member sizes, reinforcement or universal setbacks.
4. Require the applicable site survey, ground investigation, loading assumptions, structural system, material specification, governing standards and engineer inputs before representing structural adequacy. Recheck current primary standards and manufacturer documents for the actual project and date. A public standards listing supplies metadata, not the equations or full licensed standard.
5. Use stable element identifiers and one measured geometry source for 2D, 3D, schedules and quantities. Map plan y to Three z; use metres internally and explicit millimetres on dimensions/DXF. Preserve floor heights, openings, passage widths, stair landings and aligned elevator shaft voids. Flag unsupported or colliding elements instead of silently moving them.
6. Track technical state per discipline and revision: missing inputs, provisional model, awaiting professional review, reviewed with evidence. Store who reviewed which revision, date, supplied report and scope. Invalidate affected reviews and exports when their inputs change. Do not turn a checkbox, AI response or geometric pass into a professional approval.
7. Keep feng shui as an optional cultural recommendation. Preserve the customer's per-floor room choices; require the customer's explicit decision before changing a layout. Evaluate measured accessibility and circulation separately from feng shui preferences.
8. Keep unconfirmed structural quantities and prices visibly provisional or excluded. Record SKU, unit, quote source/date, VAT, labour and transport assumptions. Propagate geometry/material revision changes to affected estimates; do not convert missing prices to zero.
9. Verify a meaningful project spanning at least two floors: surveyed or customer-measured irregular parcel, an offset opening, a stair/elevator void and a supplied column/beam. Check bounds, identifiers, labels, units and matching revisions across drawing/3D/export; reject invalid edits without changing history. Check review invalidation and retain unconfirmed input states. Use mocked paid APIs and affected tests only.
10. Deliver the current architectural package, engineering input checklist, unresolved issues and evidence of checks. State which outputs are architectural concepts and which contain reviewed engineering inputs. Do not certify construction safety, fire compliance or permits from an AI model or geometry checks.

Use Vietnamese product labels. Add no credential or paid-service integration as a side effect of this workflow. Use exact file ownership for parallel work and integrate shared state/build/export contracts sequentially.
